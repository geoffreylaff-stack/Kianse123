    What this does

    This page holds an offline index of works scored for the oboe family: oboe, English horn (cor anglais), oboe d’amore, oboe da caccia, bass oboe and heckelphone. Search a composer and every matching work is listed with its exact scoring — “two oboes, english horn”, “three oboes”, and so on.

    Nothing is installed and nothing is sent anywhere: the index ships with the page and all searching happens inside your browser.

    Working somewhere without wifi? Download a single-file offline copy — the whole index in one HTML file that runs straight from your desktop.

    Catalogue data derived from IMSLP / Petrucci Music Library, combined with a hand-checked layer for orchestral works. Instrumentation should be confirmed against a published score before hire or performance.
